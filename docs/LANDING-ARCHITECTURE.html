Archived document-camera landing experience

Superseded by the specimen-led main page. The application now opens directly into the playground. The source and assets described below are retained for reuse but are inactive.

Implemented locally — 5 October 2026.
The supplied model retains its original yellow and gray materials. Its tall, angled composition follows the corrected layout. The black circular lens on the angled neck is the entry target.

Open Jelly Study, choose a camera move, and click the lens. All three moves reveal the Peach playground. Use the Jelly Study name in the playground header to return.

Experience and camera motion

VariationMotion
Lens glideA continuous perspective shift and approach along the lens normal.
OverheadA horizontal top view followed by a curved approach into the same lens.
Arc inA wider curved approach that closes in on the lens.

Camera movement is independent of jelly physics. A transition owns the view until the playground takes over; the playground keeps toolbar-only zoom. Reduced motion uses a brief fade. Entry also works with the keyboard, and the transition can be skipped.

Asset fidelity and future interaction

The original 66.3 MB GLB is retained in attached_assets/document-camera-original.glb. The served 44.7 MB copy uses lossless mesh compression. All 48 decoded geometry buffers are verified byte-for-byte, and original texture bytes and materials are retained. There is no triangle simplification or color replacement. The original scan can still show surface imperfections at extreme close range, and the study's lighting differs from Blender.

scripts/prepare-document-camera.mjs reproduces the asset and writes docs/document-camera-asset.json. Its 12 generic source meshes are preserved, but they do not define reliable mechanical joints. Future arm/lamp articulation needs an authored rig or deliberate part mapping. An empty group and a text-safe overlay region reserve space for future jelly slices; none are added yet.

Responsive behavior and verification

The desktop model fills almost the full viewport height. Portrait compositions fit the whole model under the title; the caption remains on screen while the hit target stays on the lens. Layouts were checked at 390×844 and 320×568, with a single canvas and no document overflow. Actual iOS/Android hardware remains untested.

All three entry variations were checked in Chrome and reach the Peach playground. Settings persist across return and re-entry. The no-renderer path provides an Enter the study button and a labeled software still preview with simulation controls disabled. All 10 CPU tests, the full workspace typecheck, and production build pass. Camera-path tests check finite continuous poses, non-singular orientation, the horizontal overhead stage, and exact convergence on the lens.
